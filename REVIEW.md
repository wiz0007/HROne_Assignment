### Non-defects inspected and justified
- **Absence of ID in attendance records**: The OpenAPI contract specifies that attendance records do not have an `id` or MongoDB `_id` field; records are addressed by their natural key `(emp_code, date)`. The starter's attempt to attach `id = str(res.inserted_id)` was the defect, not the absence of an ID in the schema.
- **Overnight shifts where `shift_end <= shift_start`**: While typically `end <= start` might look like an invalid time range, in this business domain it explicitly designates overnight shifts (e.g. 22:00 to 06:00) per Rule R1.
- **Read-only derived fields**: Fields such as `work_hours`, `late_minutes`, `overtime_minutes`, and `half_day` are omitted from request models because they are computed exclusively by the server and are strictly read-only. Client-supplied values are ignored per OpenAPI instructions.
- **Extra fields in employee creation**: Pydantic v2 ignores unmodeled request attributes by default (`extra='ignore'`), complying with the contract's mandate to safely ignore unexpected client payload attributes (such as derived metrics).
